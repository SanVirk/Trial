Nervous patients

Nervous about the dentist?

You're not alone, and you won't be judged. Many of our patients felt the same before their first visit.

  • We'll talk first, with no examination until you're ready
  • Agree a stop signal and take breaks whenever you need them
  • We explain each step before we do it, at your pace

Spread the cost, and put prevention first

How to join

Joining takes a few minutes

  1. Get in touch

    Call us on 01909 472477 or pop in to the practice to talk through which plan suits you.

  2. Complete your agreement

    Call in to fill in a short care plan agreement and a Direct Debit mandate.

  3. Monthly payments

    Payments are collected monthly by Direct Debit.

  4. You're covered

    Your cover starts on the 1st of the month.

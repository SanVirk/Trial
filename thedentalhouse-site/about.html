Our practice

A family practice that takes time for you

The Dental House is an independent, private dental practice in Worksop. We look after individuals and families of all ages, and we believe good dentistry starts with listening.

Book an appointmentCall 01909 472477

Our philosophy

Good dentistry is personal

We know that a visit to the dentist isn't everyone's idea of a good morning. That's why we work hard to make The Dental House feel calm, friendly and unhurried, from the moment you walk in.

Every patient is different, so your care is planned around you. We'll talk you through what we find, explain your options in plain English and give you a written estimate before any treatment begins. You're involved in every decision, and you're always welcome to ask questions.

Our focus is on keeping your mouth healthy for life. That means prevention first, careful and conservative treatment when it's needed, and regular care that fits around you and your family.

The way we work

Personal care

You'll see familiar faces who know you and your history, and who remember what matters to you.

Decisions made together

We explain what we see and set out your options, costs and alternatives. You decide what happens next.

At your pace

Nothing is rushed. If you'd like time to think, or would rather take treatment in smaller steps, that's fine with us.

A calm atmosphere

A friendly welcome, a quiet waiting room and a team who take time to put you at ease.

The team

Experienced people you'll get to know

Our dentists, hygienists and therapists are supported by an experienced team of dental nurses and receptionists. Every clinician is registered with the General Dental Council.

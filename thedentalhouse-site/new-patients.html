New patients

A warm welcome to The Dental House

Joining a new practice should feel easy. Here's what to expect at your first visit, what to bring, and how to register.

Book an appointmentCall 01909 472477

How to register

Three simple steps

  1. Get in touch

    Call 01909 472477 or send us an enquiry. We'll find an appointment that suits you.

  2. Tell us about you

    We'll ask you to complete a medical history form when you arrive, or before your visit.

  3. Your first visit

    A relaxed, thorough consultation with your dentist, and time for any questions.

Your first visit

What to expect

  • A friendly welcome and a chance to tell us about any worries or goals
  • A thorough examination of your teeth, gums and mouth, including a mouth cancer check
  • X-rays if they're needed
  • A clear explanation of what we find, and your options
  • A written treatment plan and estimate before any treatment begins

What to bring

Bring along

  • A list of any medicines you take, including inhalers and creams
  • Details of any medical conditions or allergies
  • Your previous dentist's details, if you have them
  • A payment card, as payment is due on completion of treatment
  • Please arrive a few minutes early to settle in

FAQs

Questions new patients ask

Are you taking on new patients?

Yes, we welcome new patients of all ages. Call us on 01909 472477 or send an enquiry and we'll arrange your first visit. VERIFY

Do you offer NHS treatment?

The Dental House is a private practice. You can join one of our dental care plans or pay for treatment as you go. VERIFY: confirm no NHS contract

Do you see children?

Yes. We're a family practice, and we love seeing children from an early age so that visiting the dentist feels normal and relaxed. Please ask about care plan options for children. VERIFY

How much will my first visit cost?

Please see our fees page and the private fee scale (PDF), or call us and we'll be happy to explain. You'll always get a written estimate before any treatment.

How do I pay?

Payment is due on completion of treatment, by cash or card VERIFY. Care plan fees are paid monthly by Direct Debit.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice so we can offer the appointment to someone else.

I'm nervous. Can you help?

Absolutely. Let us know when you book and we'll make sure there's time to talk first. Read more on our nervous patients page.

Is there parking and step-free access?

Pay-and-display parking is available at Worksop Train Station or Matalan (Eastgate), and in the Halfords/Screwfix car park. Disabled access is at the rear of the practice.

Coughs, colds and respiratory illness

If you're feeling unwell with a cough, cold, flu-like symptoms or a high temperature, please don't come to the practice. Call us on 01909 472477 and we'll happily rearrange your appointment.

Face coverings are optional. You're welcome to wear one if you'd prefer.

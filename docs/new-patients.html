The 16-point health assessment

A proper look — not a quick glance

Prevention is at the heart of what we do. Rather than simply treating problems as they arise, our detailed assessment helps us spot issues early and keep your natural teeth for as long as possible.

  • A thorough check of your gums, cheeks, jaw, tongue and teeth
  • Soft-tissue and oral health screening for early peace of mind
  • Bite, wear and TMJ assessment where relevant
  • A tailored prevention plan for you and your family

Nervous or anxious?

You’re in good hands. We’re known for putting anxious patients at ease — working at a speed you’re comfortable with, offering appointments during quieter hours, and using gentle, pain-free injection techniques. As one patient said: “As a nervous patient I was put at ease… all the staff are helpful and friendly.”

Dental emergencies

In pain or had an accident? Please call us as early in the day as possible on 0121 475 3545 and we’ll do our best to see you quickly. We keep time aside for urgent care and will get you comfortable as soon as we can.

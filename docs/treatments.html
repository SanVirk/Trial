General & preventive care

Health & hygiene

Routine examinations, professional cleaning and our 16-point health assessment keep your gums healthy and your natural teeth strong. Our hygiene team are gentle, thorough and full of practical advice tailored to your routine.

Pain-free injections

Comfort is everything. Our gentle, pain-free injection techniques mean treatment needn’t be something to dread — a big reason nervous patients feel so at ease with us.

TMJ & jaw disorders

Jaw pain, clicking or headaches can stem from problems with the temporomandibular joint. We assess your bite and offer treatments such as bite appliances to relieve discomfort.

White fillings

Tooth-coloured composite fillings repair decay and replace old metal fillings discreetly, blending naturally with your smile while keeping your teeth strong.

Crowns, bridges & dentures

Crowns & bridges

Beautifully crafted crowns restore damaged teeth, while bridges fill gaps left by missing teeth — both hard-wearing and natural in appearance.

Dentures

Comfortable, natural-looking partial and full dentures, expertly made by our clinical dental technician for a secure, confident fit.

Restoring your smile

“Thank you for the excellent work you did on my crowns,” one patient told us. We take real pride in restorations that look and feel just right.

Dental implants

The gold-standard, permanent solution for missing teeth.

Dental implants replace the root of a missing tooth with a small titanium post, topped with a natural-looking crown, bridge or secured denture. The result is stable, long-lasting and looks just like your own teeth — no more worrying about loose dentures.

  • Replace one tooth, several teeth, or stabilise a full denture
  • Protects your jawbone and preserves your facial shape
  • Cared for like natural teeth — built to last for years

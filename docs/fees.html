Your first visit

What a new patient visit includes

Your initial appointment is thorough and unhurried — up to 30 minutes — and forms the foundation of your care with us.

  • Full 16-point health assessment of teeth, gums and soft tissues
  • Discussion of your concerns, goals and medical history
  • Any necessary X-rays, explained as we go
  • A personalised plan with clear, written costs

A detailed price guide is available on request and at your consultation — just ask reception on 0121 475 3545.

Our philosophy

Confidence and reassurance, every visit

“Our honest, open and positive approach to dental wellbeing provides patients with confidence and reassurance.”

West Heath Dental Practice is a private practice serving the Birmingham area with quality care and an established, family-centred approach. We’re proud of our continuity: some members of our team have been here for more than two decades, and many of our patients have been with us for 25, 38, even 45 years.

That longevity is no accident. We take the time to understand each patient, work at a pace you’re comfortable with, and focus on preventing problems rather than simply treating them — helping you keep your natural teeth for life.

What we stand for

Care that’s ethical, gentle & built to last

Prevention first

We aim to prevent oral health problems, not merely treat them — building confidence that your teeth and gums are well cared for, with reduced long-term costs.

Genuinely caring

A friendly, reassuring team and pain-free techniques. We’re known for putting nervous patients at ease and working at a speed that suits you.

Family for generations

We promote good dental awareness in children to support lifelong tooth retention, and care for whole families — often across several generations.

Our signature 16-point health check

Every new patient receives a detailed, unhurried assessment covering your gums, cheeks, jaw, tongue and teeth — not just a quick look. It’s how we catch issues early and tailor care around you.

  • Up to 30 minutes for your first visit, so nothing is rushed.
  • A full oral health screen including soft-tissue and gum checks.
